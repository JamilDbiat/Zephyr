Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2025  •  All sales are handled through Discord.

Our Policy

At Delay Services, we strive to ensure every transaction is smooth and satisfactory. Due to the digital nature of Roblox items, all sales are generally considered final. However, we do review refund requests on a case-by-case basis.

Eligible for Refund

  • You paid but never received your item(s) after a reasonable wait time.
  • The item delivered was significantly different from what was listed.
  • A technical error occurred that resulted in a duplicate charge.
  • You contacted us within 24 hours of your purchase.

Not Eligible for Refund

  • You changed your mind after the item was successfully delivered.
  • Your Roblox account was banned after receiving the item.
  • You violated our Terms of Service prior to or during the transaction.
  • More than 24 hours have passed since the transaction was completed.
  • Chargebacks filed without contacting support first will result in a permanent ban.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, open a support ticket in our Discord server. Please include your order details, username, and a description of the issue. Our team will respond within 24–48 hours.

Important Notice

Delay Services reserves the right to deny any refund request that does not meet the criteria above. Abuse of the refund process will result in a permanent account ban.
